Percentage Calculator

Percentages, share of a total and percentage change.

1. What is X% of Y?

2. X is what percent of Y?

3. Percentage increase / decrease

Formulas

  • X% of Y = (X ÷ 100) × Y
  • X is what % of Y = (X ÷ Y) × 100
  • Change = ((New − Old) ÷ |Old|) × 100

Home › Discount Calculator

Discount Calculator

Final price after discounts and optional tax.

How it works

Sale price = Original × (1 − Discount ÷ 100). A second discount is taken off the already reduced price, and tax is applied last.

Formula
